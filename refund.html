Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Cancelling an order

  • Before the shop accepts: you can cancel from the Orders tab at no charge.
  • After the shop accepts: contact the shop or email us. The shop may cancel if the item is no longer available.
  • Shop rejects or cancels: the order is cancelled and no fee is charged.
  • Patang Go may cancel orders that look fraudulent or break the rules.

Problems with your order

If an item does not arrive, is the wrong item, or arrives damaged, report it within 24 hours of delivery by email to support@patang-go.com, with your order details and a photo where possible. Do not tap “I received it” for an order you have not received.

Refunds

  • Online payment is not yet available. For now, payment is arranged directly between you and the shop, and any refund is settled with the shop.
  • When online payment is added, payments will be held until you confirm delivery. Approved refunds will go back to the original payment method within 7 to 10 working days.
  • Fees are not charged on cancelled or refunded orders.

Contact

Email support@patang-go.com for any cancellation or refund question.
