Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Who we are

Patang Go is a mobile marketplace that connects kite buyers with verified kite shops in Lahore during the Basant season. This policy explains what information the Patang Go app and website collect, why, and what you can do about it. Contact us at support@patang-go.com.

Information we collect

  • Account: your name and email address from Google Sign-In.
  • Orders: the phone number, delivery address and note you enter when you order, and the items, prices and status of each order.
  • Shops (sellers only): shop name, owner name, phone and WhatsApp numbers, address, area, city, shop location, government license number, and a photo of your government certificate.
  • Items: names, descriptions, prices, stock and photos that sellers add.
  • Location: your phone's location, only when you allow it, to show the nearest shops or to save a shop's position. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Reports: reports you send about a shop.

How we use it

  • To run your account and show you the right screens.
  • To pass your order, phone number and address to the shop you order from so it can deliver.
  • To let our team verify sellers' government certificates before their shop goes live.
  • To keep the marketplace safe: blocking banned items, reviewing reports, and suspending shops or accounts that break the rules.
  • To calculate fees and keep records of orders.

Who can see your information

  • Buyers see a shop's name, area, approximate location (to about 1 km) and items. A shop's phone, WhatsApp number and exact address are not shown to buyers; for a pickup order, the shop's address is shown to that buyer only after the shop accepts the order.
  • Shops see the name, phone, address and note of buyers who order from them.
  • Patang Go admins can see accounts, shops, certificates, orders and reports to run and protect the service.
  • A seller's certificate photo is visible only to that seller and to Patang Go admins.
  • We do not sell your information and do not show advertising.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), operated by Google. Data is protected by access rules so that each person can read only what their role needs.

Payments

Online payment is not yet available in the app. When it is added, payments will be handled by a licensed payment provider, and Patang Go will not store your full card or bank account details.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. Order records may be kept for as long as needed for accounting and legal requirements.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from the app: open the menu (⋮) and choose “Delete account”. This removes your account and, for sellers, your shop, items and certificate. You can also ask us to delete it by emailing support@patang-go.com.

Children

Patang Go is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
