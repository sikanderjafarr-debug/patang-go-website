How it works Sell on Patang Go

Android app coming soon on Google Play.

Open only during Basant, when the government allows it

Patang Go is a seasonal app. It works only for the days that the Government of Punjab permits the sale and purchase of kites.

  • The app opens for buying and selling when the government allows kite sales for Basant.
  • It closes as soon as the government stops kite sales.
  • It does not operate after Basant. No orders can be placed outside the permitted days.
Services

What Patang Go offers

Patang Go is a mobile app that connects buyers with verified kite shops in Lahore during Basant.

  • Verified shop listing

    Kite shops checked by our team list their kites, cotton string and spools with photos and prices.

    Free for shops; 6% on completed orders

  • Home delivery orders

    Order from a nearby shop and get it delivered to your home.

    4% service fee + shop's delivery fee

  • Shop pickup orders

    Pick up from the shop yourself or send a rider. Handed over only with your 4-digit pickup code.

    4% service fee

  • Multi-shop cart

    Order from up to 3 shops at once. Each shop sends its own order.

    4% service fee per order

  • Patang Baaz pass

    Standard pass: silver badge, name on the leaderboard, 5 pecha challenges a day.

    From Rs 1,500 (one Basant day) to Rs 4,000 (3 days)

  • Top Patang Baaz pass

    Premium pass: gold badge, unlimited pecha challenges, your photo on the leaderboard and the Premium Wall.

    From Rs 2,500 (one Basant day) to Rs 6,000 (3 days)

  • Pecha Challenge

    On Basant days, challenge pass holders within 1 km to a friendly kite match. 18+ only, cotton string only, no betting.

    Included with a pass

  • Sponsored ads

    Brands can sponsor the Basant season in the app. Every ad is marked “Sponsored”.

    Packages on request
For buyers

How an order works

Three steps from the shop's shelf to your rooftop.

  1. Browse verified shops

    See kites, string and spools with photos and prices. The nearest shops show first.

  2. Place your order

    Choose the quantity and enter your phone and address. The shop accepts it and sends it out.

  3. Confirm when it arrives

    Tap “I received it” once the items are in your hands. That completes the order.

For sellers

Reach buyers across Lahore

Every shop on Patang Go is checked by our team before it goes live. To join you need:

  • A Google account to sign in
  • Your shop name, address and location
  • Your government kite-selling license or permit number
  • A clear photo of the certificate issued to you

Once approved, you add your own items with photos and manage orders from the app.

Fees

Seller fee, on each completed order6%
Buyer service fee, added at checkout4%

Example: a kite order of Rs 1,000. The buyer pays Rs 1,040 and the shop receives Rs 940.

No fee is charged on cancelled orders. There is no sign-up fee.

Safety

Only legal, safe string

Patang Go follows the government's rules for Basant. Listings with banned material are blocked automatically, and shops that sell them are suspended.
